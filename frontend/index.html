About CDVH Platform

Disaster Management Center Elevating coordination with Advanced AI solutions.

During disasters, organizing help is difficult. Information is scattered, and finding volunteers with the right skills in real-time is a challenge. CDVH solves this by integrating Machine Learning for task matching and Natural Language Processing to instantly understand emergency messages.
